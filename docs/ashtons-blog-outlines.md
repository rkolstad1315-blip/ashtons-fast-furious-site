# Blog post outlines

Six launch candidates for ticket 15. Each answers a question a local dog owner would actually search, then points to booking. Write them from the owner's real answers: anything marked [CONFIRM] has to be answered before that post can publish, and the build leaves out any post that still has a placeholder.

## Rules for every post

- **No keyword overlap with a page.** The service, pricing, safety, daycare, and town pages each own their keyword. A post that targets the same search competes with the page, so posts take adjacent questions instead.
- **No invented numbers.** Exercise minutes, temperatures, and health claims come from the owner's own practice or from a linked source (AVMA, ASPCA, AKC, or a vet). Medical topics end with "check with your vet first."
- **Local in the specifics, not in stuffed phrases.** Name real things: Onslow County humidity, base housing, rural roads in Duplin County. Use "Jacksonville, NC" with the state, never "Jacksonville" alone.
- **One call to action at the end:** book the free first session, or call or text 910-967-0205.
- **Length:** 700 to 1,200 words. Answer the title question in the first two sentences.
